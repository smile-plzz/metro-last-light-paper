## 1. Search Protocol Architecture

The literature search is structured to capture three primary domains:
1. **Domain A: Direct Scholarship on *Metro: Last Light* and the *Metro* Franchise** (Theology, ethics, wicked problems, antiquity, memory).
2. **Domain B: Game Studies, Ludology, and Interactive Ethics** (Implicit morality systems, procedural rhetoric, ethical gameplay, ludonarrative dissonance, player complicity, post-apocalyptic dystopias).
3. **Domain C: Cultural Memory, Political Theory, and Post-Soviet Spatiality** (Nostalgia in ruins, totalitarian reproduction, alterity and Levinasian ethics, the Anthropocene and post-nuclear ecologies).

### Target Databases & Repositories
- **Crossref & OpenAlex:** Comprehensive DOI metadata and bibliographic resolution.
- **Google Scholar:** Broad citation tracking and multidisciplinary coverage.
- **DiGRA Digital Library:** Specialized game studies conference proceedings and journals.
- **Sage Journals & Taylor & Francis Online:** *Games and Culture*, *Journal of Systematic Theology*, *Critical Studies in Media Communication*.
- **Primary Developer & Author Repositories:** 4A Games official archives, Dmitry Glukhovsky official writings, Deep Silver/PLAION technical documentation.

## 3. Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria (IC)
- **IC1 (Relevance):** Peer-reviewed scholarly papers, book chapters, or academic monographs directly addressing *Metro: Last Light*, the *Metro* universe, or core theoretical foundations (procedural rhetoric, implicit morality, post-apocalyptic ideology, cultural memory).
- **IC2 (Empirical & Primary Integrity):** Primary developer interviews, official game design documentation, published author statements, or verifiable game-text transcripts.
- **IC3 (Methodological Rigor):** Theoretical and analytical works published in recognized academic venues with verified DOIs, ISBNs, or institutional URLs.
- **IC4 (Temporal Cutoff):** Sources published up to November 3, 2026.

### Exclusion Criteria (EC)
- **EC1 (Unverified Journalism / Clickbait):** Casual gaming blogs, unsourced forum threads, or SEO-driven walkthroughs without authorial attribution.
- **EC2 (Hallucinated / Synthetic Citations):** Any citation lacking verifiable metadata, active DOI, or library catalog listing.
- **EC3 (Conflated Franchise Analysis):** Sources discussing *Metro 2033* or *Metro Exodus* without clear delineations of the specific narrative/ludic systems of *Last Light*.
- **EC4 (Purely Speculative Psychological Claims):** Non-peer-reviewed claims regarding neurological or emotional player impact lacking phenomenological or empirical grounding.
