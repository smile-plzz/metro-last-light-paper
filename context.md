## Core Goals
1. Conduct an interdisciplinary, publication-quality qualitative research study on *Metro: Last Light* (2013).
2. Investigate the central contradiction: Humanity survives physical destruction but reproduces the ideological, tribal, and violent structures that caused it.
3. Systematically analyze narrative architecture, character psychology, political factionalism, otherness (the Dark Ones), environmental survival mechanics, and the implicit morality system.
4. Target length: 7,000–9,000 words for the academic manuscript (excluding references and appendices).
5. Produce the complete suite of 16 research deliverables requested by the user, adhering strictly to the `academic-research-skills` pipeline architecture.

## Methodological Stack & Standards
- Primary Methodology: Interpretive qualitative single-case study, close ludonarrative analysis, critical discourse analysis of factions, procedural rhetoric analysis of mechanics.
- Citations: APA 7th edition, strict anti-hallucination verification (all scholarly sources must have verifiable DOIs/URLs/records).
- Technical Stack: Python 3.13 (`python-docx` for `.docx` generation, `pandas` for CSV matrices, `pymupdf`).
- Version Distinction: Primary object is *Metro: Last Light* (2013 original release); *Metro: Last Light Redux* (2014) is evaluated strictly as a comparative edition where mechanics/presentation differ. Transmedia distinctions between Glukhovsky's novels (*Metro 2033*, *Metro 2034*, *Metro 2035*) and 4A Games' titles are maintained rigorously.

## Constraints & Integrity Rules
- No fabricated references, synthetic statistics, or artificial DOIs.
- Disclose all boundaries of player-reception scholarship vs. direct interpretive textual analysis.
- Full spoiler policy active: all chapters, revelations, and both endings (*Redemption* vs. *C'est la vie*) are in scope.
- Maintain persistent research artifacts across all stages.
